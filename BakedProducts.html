Prepared Foods and Their Categories

Home | Fast Food | Baked Products

Prepared foods are ready-to-eat or heat-and-eat items designed for fast meals, and they are grouped into several major categories based on how they are stored, sold, and processed.
  • Fresh and Chilled Prepared Foods
  • Hot Deli and Prepared Meals
  • Shelf-Stable and Packaged Convenience Foods
  • Frozen Prepared Meals
  • Specialty and Medical Prepared Foods
Mixed foods improve nutrition, taste, and safety in prepared meals by blending different food categories together. Mixed foods are often consider as prepared food in multiple ways than having one meal type in a feast:
  • Better nutrition
  • Balanced energy
  • Complete proteins
  • Better taste and texture
  • More Calories (In most cases)
