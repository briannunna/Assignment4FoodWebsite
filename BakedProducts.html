Baked foods can provide staple calories that are dense when it comes to nutrition, a large amount of culinary bases, and an extremely long shelf-stability.

Baked foods can give very important carbohydrates, fats, and proteins derived from grain-based flours. Not only that, they can serve as a base to other popular foods, like pizza.

Also, they have an extended shelf life too, which can make them a great option for long term storage.
Baked products are also not only popular in today's world due to their bread bases, but also because of other things like pastries, cakes, and cookies.

Pastries are popular in today's world due mostly to their sweet taste and physical appeal to the human eye.
